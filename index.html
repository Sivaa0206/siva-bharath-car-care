What we do

Services, grouped the way the workshop is

Diesel work first, then everything a car needs to stay on the road. Labels show which side of the workshop a job belongs to.

  • Diesel

    Diesel engine repair

    Smoke, hard starting, knocking or loss of pull traced to the actual cause - injectors, pump, timing or compression - not guessed at.

  • Diesel

    Engine overhauling

    Full strip and rebuild. Block and head measured, bearings, rings, seals and gaskets renewed, then set up and run in before handover.

  • Diagnostics

    Car scanning & diagnostics

    Scanner on the port, live data read, fault codes explained in plain words - so you know what the warning light actually means.

  • Electrical

    Electrical diagnostics

    Wiring, alternator, starter, sensors and earth faults chased down with a meter. Parts get replaced because they failed, not on a hunch.

  • Electrical

    Battery replacement

    Charging system tested first, because a good battery keeps dying for a reason. New battery fitted, terminals cleaned, old one taken away.

  • Service

    General car service

    Periodic service to your vehicle's schedule: oil and filters, fuel filter or plugs, fluids, belts, tyre pressures and a road test.

  • Service

    Oil change

    Correct grade for your engine, genuine filter, leaks and levels checked, and the next-due reading written on your job card.

  • Service

    Vehicle inspection

    Buying a used car or heading on a long trip? Engine, brakes, suspension, tyres and body checked, with a written verdict you can act on.

  • Running gear

    Suspension repair

    Knocks over bumps, bounce and uneven tyre wear fixed at the source: struts, bushes, ball joints, tie rods and mountings.

  • Running gear

    Brake service

    Pads, shoes, discs, drums, cylinders and fluid - set up so the car stops straight, quietly, and holds on ghat roads.

  • Running gear

    Clutch repair

    Slipping, hard pedal or judder in traffic. Plate, cover, bearing and cylinders replaced, and pedal free-play set correctly.

  • Comfort

    AC service

    Gas top-up or leak repair, condenser and cooling coil cleaned, blower and cabin filter checked. Cold air again before summer.

  • On call

    Emergency repairs

    Broken down near Surandai? Call first - plenty of faults get solved on the phone. If not, we reach you or arrange a tow.
